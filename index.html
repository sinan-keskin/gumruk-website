Clearance Lifecycle

The Topraklar Precision Protocol

From initial document audit to border clearance and final release, our structured workflow eliminates demurrage fees and customs penalties.

01

Dossier Pre-Audit & HS Classification

Our Senior Licensed Brokers inspect commercial invoices, packing lists, bill of ladings, and technical data sheets to assign the legally accurate 12-digit GTİP tariff code.

02

Permits & Technical Filing

Direct interface with TAREKS, Ministry of Agriculture, CITES, and CE inspection bodies. Pre-clearance electronic applications submitted before the cargo enters port waters.

03

Customs Tescil & Physical Inspection

Electronic declaration registered with the Ministry of Trade. Dedicated field brokers oversee red channel physical examinations, laboratory sampling, and green channel releases.

04

Duty Settlement & Gate Release

Automated tax and fee settlement. Issuance of the electronic release slip (Fiili İthalat / Çıkış İzni) and hand-off to bonded transport fleets for prompt client delivery.
