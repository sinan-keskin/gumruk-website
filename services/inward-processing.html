Inward Processing Legislation (Import - Export Customs Brokerage) Services

Duty-free importation of manufacturing raw materials, semi-finished goods, and components under Inward Processing Authorisations (DİİB). Maximizing cash flow through legal customs duty, VAT, and trade policy exemption.

Statutory Scope & Legal Architecture

The Inward Processing Regime (Dahilde İşleme Rejimi - DİİB) governed by the Council of Ministers Decision No. 2005/8391 and Customs Law No. 4458 Articles 108–127 enables Turkish manufacturers to obtain raw materials, auxiliary components, and packaging at world market prices without paying customs duties, Value Added Tax (KDV), Special Consumption Tax (ÖTV), or additional financial charges, provided the resulting finished goods are exported within the validity period.

Failure to balance input imports against export declarations or committing errors in manufacturing loss ratios (fire oranı) triggers retroactive taxation with compensatory interest (gecikme zammı) and penalties under Article 238 of the Customs Law. Topraklar’s Inward Processing Advisory Board oversees your entire certificate lifecycle—from electronic application to Ministry certificate closure.

Key Operational Competencies:

  • DİİB Certificate Acquisition (E-İmza & DYS): Structuring project dossiers on the Ministry of Trade Export Information System (DYS), obtaining raw material quotas based on industrial capacity reports.
  • Conditional Exemption (Şartlı Muafiyet - 5100): Handling import declarations under reduced collateral rates (Indirgenmiş Teminat) for AEO/YYS or approved manufacturing status.
  • Drawback System (Geri Ödeme Sistemi - 4100): Reclaiming duties already paid upon proof of export of processed products.
  • Commitment Closure (Taahhüt Kapatma): Reconciling raw material consumption tables (İşletme Malzeme Sarfiyatı) and securing official closure letters to release millions in bank guarantee letters.

Step-by-Step Inward Processing Management

Phase 1: Capacity & Quota Modeling

Evaluation of factory Capacity Report (Kapasite Raporu). Formulation of raw material import codes vs. finished export HS codes.

Phase 2: Duty-Free Import Clearances

Execution of Regime 5100 import declarations. Placing discounted bank letters of guarantee (1% or 10%) instead of full cash duty.

Phase 3: Outward Export Synchronization

Registering Regime 3151 export declarations referencing the active DİİB certificate number. Monitoring quota depletion in real time.

Phase 4: Ministry Audit & Guarantee Dissolution

Dossier submission to the Regional Directorate of Trade. Certificate officially closed and bank letters of guarantee returned.

Required Import Dossier Checklist

Documents required to initiate DİİB management:

Valid Chamber Capacity Report (Kapasite Raporu)
Trade Registry Gazette & Signature Circular
Raw Material Input-Output Consumption Formulation
Proforma Import Invoices & Supplier Specs
Export Sales Contracts / Purchase Orders
Bank Letter of Guarantee or AEO Exemption Copy
