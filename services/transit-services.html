Transit Services

Duty-suspended cargo transit across national borders and customs territories. Complete administration of NCTS T1/T2 declarations, TIR Carnets, comprehensive corporate guarantees, and multimodal bond escorts.

Statutory Scope & Legal Architecture

Under Articles 84–92 of the Customs Law No. 4458 and the Common Transit Convention (Ortak Transit Sözleşmesi) linking Turkey with the European Union, EFTA states, the UK, and North Macedonia, commercial goods in transit may move across customs territories without being subject to customs duties, VAT, or trade policy commercial measures, under customs seal.

Operating transit requires extensive financial security. Topraklar Gümrük provides clients with our multimillion-euro Comprehensive Transit Guarantee (Kapsamlı Teminat) and Authorized Consignor (İzinli Gönderici) privileges. This enables cargo to depart directly from maritime port berths (Ambarlı, Mersin, İzmir, Derince) to internal customs houses (Erenköy, Muratbey, Ankara) or third countries (Iraq, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran, Syria) without locking up client cash reserves.

Key Operational Competencies:

  • Electronic NCTS (T1 / T2 / TR) Declarations: Automated electronic filing into the New Computerised Transit System linking all 36 European customs administrations.
  • Comprehensive Guarantee Coverage: Deploying our corporate bank guarantee envelope with the Ministry of Trade to cover high-value goods (electronics, textiles, machinery) in transit.
  • TIR Carnet Administration (IRU / TOBB): Handling electronic TIR declarations (E-TIR) and customs verification at border crossing posts (Kapıkule, Hamzabeyli, Sarp, Habur, Gürbulak).
  • Seal Integrity & Satellite GPS Tracking: Supervising physical installation of high-security customs cable seals and electronic GPS route tracking devices.

Step-by-Step Transit Execution Roadmap

Phase 1: Transit Declaration Filing

Electronic T1 declaration lodged on NCTS upon vessel discharge or border arrival. Guarantee allocated automatically.

Phase 2: Customs Seal & Route Assignment

Customs officers inspect vehicle security, apply official seals, and assign a mandatory transit route and transit deadline.

Phase 3: Corridor Transport Monitoring

Real-time GPS tracking across transit highways ensuring full adherence to designated corridors and intermediate checkpoints.

Phase 4: Destination Arrival & Discharge

Vehicle presents at destination customs office or border exit post. Seal verified intact, NCTS closed, and guarantee restored.

Required Import Dossier Checklist

Documents required to initiate NCTS / TIR transit declarations:

Commercial Transit Invoice & Packing List
CMR International Consignment Note / Bill of Lading
Vehicle Customs Approval Certificate (Uygunluk Belgesi)
NCTS T1 Transit Accompanying Document (TAD)
Driver Passport & Vehicle Registration Papers
