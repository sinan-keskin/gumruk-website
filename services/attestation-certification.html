Statutory Scope & Legal Architecture

When conducting foreign trade with non-EU and non-Hague convention territories (particularly the Gulf Cooperation Council [GCC] states, Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asia), commercial customs authorities demand that trade documents be officially attested through a rigorous chain of legalizations before import customs clearance is granted in the destination port.

Any missing chamber endorsement, governorate verification, or consular diplomatic stamp will cause cargo to be detained at foreign discharge ports, incurring severe vessel demurrage. Topraklar’s Specialized Attestation Unit handles the entire sequential bureaucratic chain—from certified notary translation and Chamber of Commerce certification to Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) and Foreign Consulate approvals in Istanbul and Ankara.

Key Operational Competencies:

  • Chamber of Commerce Approvals (İTO, İSO, EBSO): Endorsement of commercial invoices, certificates of origin, free sale certificates, and packing lists with official Chamber seals.
  • Hague Apostille Certification (Valilik / Kaymakamlık): Securing Apostille stamps under the 1961 Hague Convention for cross-border legal acceptance in over 120 signatory nations.
  • Foreign Embassy & Consular Legalization: Direct courier processing at embassies and consulates of Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Egypt, Iraq, Kuwait, Algeria, Libya, China, and others.
  • Arab-Turkish Chamber of Commerce Certification: Managing bilateral certified translations and required stamps for trade destined for Arab League nations.

Step-by-Step Legalization Chain Roadmap

Phase 1: Sworn Translation & Notary

Commercial documents translated into Arabic, English, or destination language by sworn legal translators and notarized.

Phase 2: Chamber of Commerce Seal

Endorsement at Istanbul Chamber of Commerce (İTO) or Industry (İSO) confirming trade authenticity and company registration.

Phase 3: Governorate / District Apostille

Certification at Istanbul Governorate (Valilik) or District Governorship (Kaymakamlık) validating the notary public’s signature.

Phase 4: Embassy Consular Visa

Personal submission to the destination country’s Consulate General in Istanbul or Embassy in Ankara. Visa stamped and returned.

Certificate of Origin (Chamber validated)
